# Challenge

All development uses ChatGPT with a combination of:

* GPT-5.6 Sol Medium
* GPT-5.6 Sol High
* GPT-6 Astra Light

The challenge is to only use the $20/month subscription for all development but also push major features every 2-4 days.

All builds are tagged at major feature updates and the `screenshots` directory shows the progress between revisions.

If the performance is sluggish on your browser, try your system's native browser (e.g. Safari for Mac etc), otherwise I guess you have to pay up for a fancy video card and more RAM but I digress.
